# Ambrozia Café &amp; Pâtisserie — Digital Growth Proposal

*Prepared for the owners of Ambrozia Café &amp; Pâtisserie, Viman Nagar, Pune*

Ambrozia already has what most cafés spend years chasing: a 4.8★ reputation, a
loyal Viman Nagar following, and genuinely beautiful products. This proposal is
about giving that reputation a **home online** — and then quietly automating the
work that turns visitors into regulars.

---

### 1 · A website that earns trust before the first bite

Most new customers judge a bakery online before they ever walk in. A slow,
generic page (or only a Zomato listing) makes even a great café look ordinary.

This site does the opposite. Premium design, real product photography, your
4.8★ reviews surfaced up front, and one-tap access to hours, location and
ordering — all mobile-first, because that's where your customers are. **Looking
as good as you taste is the cheapest, highest-return marketing you can own.**

### 2 · WhatsApp ordering, built in

Every "Order" button opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message, so a customer is
one tap from talking to you — no app, no friction.

**Next step:** a WhatsApp Business catalogue (menu + prices in-chat), saved quick
replies, and automated order confirmations. For higher volumes, the WhatsApp
Business API can send live order status and accept payment links automatically.

### 3 · Automated cake enquiries

The site's enquiry form already captures occasion, date, flavour and servings
and hands them to you on WhatsApp — no lead lost to a missed call.

**Automation layer:** route every enquiry into a shared inbox or Google Sheet,
send an instant "we've got your request" acknowledgement, reply with a templated
quote, and collect a deposit via a UPI/Razorpay link. Faster replies win more
celebration-cake orders — the highest-margin thing you make.

### 4 · Google Review automation to lift ratings

Ratings drive discovery: a higher, fresher star rating pushes Ambrozia above
competitors in Google Maps and search.

**How it works:** after a purchase, an automated WhatsApp/SMS thank-you includes
a **one-tap Google review link** (plus a QR code at the counter and on the bill).
Smart routing sends happy customers to Google and unhappy ones to a private
feedback form first — so you collect more 5★ reviews *and* catch problems before
they go public.

### 5 · Birthday reminders that bring customers back

A bakery's best customers have a predictable need: **every birthday and
anniversary, all year.**

Capture those dates at enquiry or checkout, and an automated WhatsApp reminder
goes out 5–7 days before with a personalised cake offer. One quiet message turns
a one-time buyer into an annual — and then monthly — regular.

### 6 · Future AI automations

As Ambrozia grows, these bolt on cleanly:

- **AI WhatsApp concierge** — answers menu, price and timing questions and takes
  orders 24/7, even after closing.
- **AI cake-design previews** — a customer describes their dream cake and sees a
  concept image instantly, then confirms with you.
- **Demand forecasting** — predicts each day's bake from past sales, festivals
  and weather to cut waste and sell-outs.
- **Auto social content** — drafts Instagram captions and posts from each new
  product photo.
- **Loyalty &amp; re-order nudges** — gentle, well-timed reminders that keep
  Ambrozia top of mind.

---

### Suggested rollout

| Phase | What goes live | Outcome |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| **1 — Launch** | This website + WhatsApp ordering + Google/maps | A premium storefront, live |
| **2 — Automate** | Enquiry routing, review automation, birthday reminders | More orders, higher rating, repeat business |
| **3 — Intelligence** | AI concierge, design previews, forecasting | Save time, sell more, waste less |

*Everything above is built on tools Ambrozia already uses — WhatsApp, Google and
Instagram — so there's nothing new for your team to learn on day one.*
